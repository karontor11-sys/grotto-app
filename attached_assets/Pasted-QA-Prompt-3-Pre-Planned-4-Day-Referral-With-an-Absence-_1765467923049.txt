Title (optional):
QA – Pre-Planned Class Period Referral (4 Days, Served vs Skipped)

Prompt text:

Please run this as a manual QA test using the existing Grotto App UI. Do not change any code. Use any test student name.

Goal: Verify that for a Pre-Planned Class Period Referral with 4 scheduled days, “Day X of 4” is based on served and completed days, not simply calendar slot position.

Step 1 – Create a 4-day Pre-Planned referral

Create a Class Period Referral with subtype Pre-Planned.

Use the schedule builder to schedule 4 separate school days for this placement.

Confirm it appears correctly on the Dashboard for the first scheduled date.

Step 2 – Day 1 (Served + Completed)

On the first scheduled Pre-Planned date:

Check In the student for the referral.

Complete the Pre-Planned day/slot as normal.

Record what the card displays for “Day X of 4”.

Step 3 – Day 2 (Absent or Not Completed)

On the second scheduled date:

Mark the student Absent, or leave the day uncompleted (do not fully serve it).

Note:

How the app records this date (absent / not served).

Whether “Day X of 4” appears and what it shows, if visible.

Step 4 – Next served day (Day 2 served)

On the next scheduled date that you fully serve and complete:

Check In the student.

Complete the Pre-Planned slot.

Record what the card displays for “Day X of 4” on this date.

Expected behavior:

First served date: should show “Day 1 of 4.”

The skipped/absent second date should not increment the served-day counter.

The next served date (the third or fourth calendar slot, depending on how you do it) should show “Day 2 of 4.”
