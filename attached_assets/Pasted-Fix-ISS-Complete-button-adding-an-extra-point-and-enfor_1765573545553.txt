Fix ISS Complete button adding an extra point (and enforce proper collapse/inactive state)

We have a bug in the ISS Dashboard completion flow.

Current Incorrect Behavior

For an ISS daily record:

Staff adds points up to the required threshold (e.g., 10/10 for a full day, or N/N for partial).

The Complete button becomes available.

When staff clicks Complete, the system adds an extra point to the daily points total (e.g., 10 becomes 11) instead of simply completing the day.

The record does not reliably “close out” into the collapsed, inactive completed display.

Expected Behavior

When the Complete button is pressed:

Do not add or modify points at all.
Completion must be a pure status transition, not a point-award action.

Mark the day/record as Completed immediately (set completed status + completed timestamp).

The Dashboard card should immediately:

Collapse to the completed, minimal view (Student name + red Completed circle)

Become inactive / non-interactive (no expanding, no editing, no point changes)

Completed records must remain visible on the correct date for historical reference, but should not be editable.

Requirements / Guardrails

Do not change database schema unless absolutely necessary.

Do not alter the points system rules except to remove the unintended extra point being added on completion.

This must work for both:

Full Day ISS (10 periods/points)

Partial Day ISS (N periods/points based on start/end)

What to Investigate and Fix

Find where the Complete button handler is calling any function that:

increments points, logs a point, or recalculates points in a way that adds an extra point.

Ensure the Complete handler does NOT call the “add behavior/point” logic path.

If the Complete handler currently “re-saves” points, ensure it saves the existing total unchanged (and ideally caps it at required points if needed for display consistency, but do not add a new point event).

Acceptance Tests

Full Day ISS:

Reach exactly 10/10 points.

Click Complete.

Points must remain 10/10 (no 11).

Card collapses to completed (red circle) and is non-expandable.

Partial Day ISS (e.g., 3–10 = 8):

Reach exactly 8/8 points.

Click Complete.

Points remain 8/8 (no extra).

Card collapses to completed (red circle) and is non-expandable.

Refresh page and navigate away/back to the date:

Completed card remains collapsed + inactive.
