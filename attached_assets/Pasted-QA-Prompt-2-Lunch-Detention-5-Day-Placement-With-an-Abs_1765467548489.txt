Title (optional):
QA – Lunch Detention 5-Day Session (Served Days and Extension After Absence)

Prompt text:

Please run this as a manual QA test using the existing Grotto App UI. Do not change any code. Use any test student name.

Goal: Verify that for a 5-day Lunch Detention, “Day X of 5” is based on served days (present + completed), and that an Absent day does not advance X, even if the schedule extends.

Step 1 – Create the placement

Create a 5-day Lunch Detention placement for a test student, starting today (or the next convenient school day).

Confirm it appears on the Dashboard.

Step 2 – Day 1 (Present + Completed)

On Day 1:

Check In the student for Lunch Detention.

Complete the session for that day as you normally would.

Record what the card displays for “Day X of 5”.

Step 3 – Day 2 (Present + Completed)

On Day 2:

Check In again.

Complete the day as normal.

Record what the card displays for “Day X of 5”.

Step 4 – Day 3 (Absent)

On Day 3:

Mark the student Absent for Lunch Detention.

Note:

What does the app display for that day?

Does the placement’s schedule extend (if visible) to add an extra date?

Step 5 – Next served day (Day 3 served)

On the next day the student actually serves Lunch Detention (the first school day after the absence):

Check In.

Complete the Lunch Detention session.

Record what the card displays for “Day X of 5”.

Expected behavior:

Day 1 served: should show “Day 1 of 5”.

Day 2 served: should show “Day 2 of 5”.

Day 3 (Absent): should not advance X; it may extend the schedule, but not count as served.

Next served day after the absence: should show “Day 3 of 5”, even if it is the 4th calendar date.
