Fix the 1-day Lunch Detention Absent refresh bug where the Complete button reappears after a page refresh. In render_lunch_detention_card, stop treating “DailyLog exists” as “checked in.” Instead, compute is_checked_in from the DailyLog’s actual checkedIn boolean, and set is_present accordingly. Then make a minimal safety guard so that if the day is marked Absent for a 1-day Lunch Detention, the Complete button is disabled (so the day can only be closed via the Absent Yes/No flow). Change no other UI or business logic.
