PRESERVE MULTI-DAY SESSION STRUCTURE
(Stabilize “Day X of Y Session” Despite Partials, Make-ups, or Skips)
⭐ PROMPT — Stabilize Day Labels & Multi-Day Session Structure

Please update the ISS Session logic so that multi-day ISS Sessions ALWAYS preserve their original “shape,” such as:

“3-Day ISS Session”

“4-Day ISS Session”

“5-Day ISS Session”

This structure must not change regardless of:

late arrivals

early departures

partial days

converted partial days

skipped calendar days

make-up days after the last scheduled day

This section focuses ONLY on the labeling and counting of ISS days, not the period math (already complete).

🔹 A. Day Labels Must Always Display as “Day X of Y ISS Session”

The Session should always display the assigned number of days:

iss_days_assigned
Example: if 4 days were assigned → always show “4-Day ISS Session”.

For each day the student is actually checked in:

Day 1 → “Day 1 of 4 ISS Session”

Day 2 → “Day 2 of 4 ISS Session”

Day 3 → “Day 3 of 4 ISS Session”

Day 4 → “Day 4 of 4 ISS Session”

Do not increment the day count based on calendar days.

Only increment when the student is checked in for ISS.

This ensures:

No-show days are skipped (correct behavior).

Weekends naturally don’t count (correct behavior).

Only days with real attendance count.

🔹 B. If a Full Day becomes Partial → The “Day #” stays the same.

Converting a Full Day to Partial (from Section 2) must NOT change:

the day number

the total day count

the “Day X of Y” label

Only periods_covered and remaining_periods are affected.

🔹 C. Make-Up Days (after scheduled days end)

If the student still owes periods after the last scheduled ISS day:

The Session remains:
“4-Day ISS Session (Active – Needs Make-Up Periods)”

Make-up days:

Do NOT increment the day counter.

Do NOT create “Day 5.”

Instead, treat them as “make-up time attached to Day 4.”

🔹 D. Required Display on Dashboard ISS Card

The ISS card for an active Session must show BOTH:

1. Day Label:

Day {current_day} of {iss_days_assigned} ISS Session

AND

2. Period Progress:

Remaining Periods Needed: {remaining_periods}

Both should update every time a day is checked in or completed.

🔹 E. Ensure Day Count Uses Only ISS Check-In Events

Use this logic to increment the Session day count:

iss_day_number = number of completed ISS calendar days


Where a “completed ISS calendar day” is defined as:

A Partial Day completed
OR

A Full Day completed
OR

A Converted Partial Day completed

Not:

weekends

skipped days

absences

future scheduled days

make-up partial days

Make-up days should NOT have a day number.
