1. Compute servedPeriodsForThisDay when “Complete Day” is clicked

points (which already matches periods based on your existing logic)

2. Recalculate servedPeriodsTotal and periodsRemaining

After saving the daily entry:

periodsRemaining = max(requiredTotalPeriods - servedPeriodsTotal, 0)

ISS: Required {requiredTotalPeriods} periods | Served {servedPeriodsTotal} | Remaining {periodsRemaining}
