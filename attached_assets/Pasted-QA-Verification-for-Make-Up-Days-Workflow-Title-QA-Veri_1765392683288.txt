QA / Verification for Make-Up Days Workflow

Title: QA – Verify ISS Make-Up Days Workflow and Final Display

Please run the following QA scenarios to confirm that the make-up days workflow behaves correctly.

Use test students like Buddy Holly, Jane Doe, and John Smith.

Scenario 1 – 3-Day ISS, One Missed Period (Simple Make-Up)

Create a 3-day full-day ISS placement for Buddy Holly:

original_day_count = 3.

session_total_periods_required = 30 (3 × 10).

For Days 1 and 2:

Complete each day normally (full-day):

After Day 1: served = 10, remaining = 20.

After Day 2: served = 20, remaining = 10.

For Day 3:

Treat it as partial attendance in a way that results in only 9 periods being served instead of 10:

For example, adjust schedule or completion such that:

After completing Day 3, session_periods_served = 29, session_periods_remaining = 1.

After completing the last original day:

Confirm that should_offer_makeup_days(placement) returns true.

Confirm the UI shows:
“Add make-up days? The student still has 1 period remaining to serve.”

Click Yes, add make-up days:

Add one make-up day for a future date with exactly 1 period scheduled.

Complete that make-up date:

Confirm:

session_periods_served = 30.

session_periods_remaining = 0.

In the Completed placement view:

Main label still shows: “3 days of ISS”.

Note indicates something like:

“Make-up required: 1 additional day (1 period) beyond original 3-day ISS assignment.”

Scenario 2 – Multiple Make-Up Days Needed

Create a 2-day ISS for Jane Doe, with both days intended as full (10 periods each):

original_day_count = 2.

session_total_periods_required = 20.

On Day 1:

Complete with only 6 periods served (e.g., partial-day scenario).

On Day 2:

Complete with only 6 periods served.

After all original days are completed:

session_periods_served = 12.

session_periods_remaining = 8.

Confirm:

Make-up prompt appears: “Add make-up days? The student still has 8 periods remaining to serve.”

Add two make-up days:

Make-Up Day A: 4 periods.

Make-Up Day B: 4 periods.

Complete both make-up days:

Final:

session_periods_served = 20.

session_periods_remaining = 0.

Final display:

Main label: “2 days of ISS”.

Note: indicates 2 make-up days, 8 make-up periods.

Scenario 3 – User Chooses “No, not now”

Using John Smith, create a 3-day ISS and deliberately under-serve periods on the original days so that session_periods_served < session_total_periods_required after Day 3.

When prompted “Add make-up days?” choose No, not now.

Confirm:

No new scheduled dates are created.

session_periods_served and session_periods_remaining remain as-is.

The placement remains in an “incomplete” state (periods still remaining).

Later, navigate back to the placement and complete another original or make-up-eligible action that triggers the condition again if appropriate; confirm the prompt can reappear until the requirement is satisfied.

Scenario 4 – Regression Checks

ISS without Make-Up Needed:

Create a 2-day full ISS where each day is fully served (10 periods).

After Day 2:

session_periods_served = 20.

session_periods_remaining = 0.

Confirm:

No make-up prompt appears.

No make-up dates are created.

Non-ISS Placements:

Create Lunch Detention and Class Period Referral placements.

Confirm:

No “Add make-up days?” prompt ever appears.

Their scheduling and completion behavior are unchanged.

If all scenarios pass exactly as described, the Make-Up Days Workflow for Multiday ISS can be considered ✅ complete and consistent with the periods logic from #2 and #3.
